# Hypersubjectivity: perspective, linkage, and candidate novelty

Status: **[FRAME] finite research contract**. `[FORM]` would require a proposition closed by a stated derivation in its own formal scope; `[FRAME]` is an explicit construction with unresolved obligations; `[OPEN]` marks an unresolved question; `[HYPER]` marks a proposed cross-level claim whose interfaces must be supplied. The tags are classifications, not evidence. The finite records below are dimensionless computational claims, not measurements of experience.

## Field boundary

Tyler proposes that realities loop and close themselves, that consciousness links hypergeometric, abstract, possibility, surreal, and base reality, and that consciousness brings forth novelties from hyperobjects. Hyperobjectivity is proposed to own the object side: how a hyperobject instantiates a hyperform and remains identifiable across presentations. **Hypersubjectivity owns the subject side:** when a first-person standpoint is actually situated with respect to a presented hyperobject, how a subject links reality presentations, and when that relation contributes a new cross-level coherence.

**[FRAME] Candidate definition:** Hypersubjectivity studies first-person relations among subjects, hyperobjects, and situated realities under explicit mechanisms and identity-preserving bridges. It must distinguish the *capacity to be seen from a first perspective* from an *actual perspective*, and distinguish an actual perspective from consciousness, a cross-reality link, or novelty. It does not reduce objectivity to agreement among subjects or assert that every hyperobject must be conscious.

Hyperethics' current entity layer treats subjectifiability as a capacity; its consciousness layer has mind, conscious medium, thought, and reality constructions with their own premises. The existing layer does not supply a hyperobject type or a five-kind cross-reality linkage operator. This repository cites that work as a boundary and cannot silently import a conclusion. Neither a first-person record nor a self-report is an instrument measuring consciousness in another entity. Any empirical interpretation requires its own observation protocol and exclusions.

## Finite link contract

The executable frame declares subjects, object-presentation references, actual perspective records, and link claims. A perspective connects one subject to one referenced object presentation and carries an experience-claim reference. A candidate link names one subject, two distinct actual perspectives, and a mechanism-claim reference. The finite check reports `typed_status=PASS` only when both perspectives belong to that subject, their object presentations are present, their reality-kind labels differ, and the mechanism reference exists. An object's mere *subjectifiability* creates no perspective or link.

`consciousness_status` and `object_continuity_status` remain `UNKNOWN` even if the input contains claims with those names. A string reference is not a checked Hyperethics derivation or a sound Hyperobjectivity transport. `typed_status=FAIL` rejects malformed candidate links; it does not refute Tyler's broader ontology. A `PASS` is conditional on all supplied finite records and can be overturned by invalidated source claims.

This separation yields concrete countermodels:

1. A subject is declared subjectifiable but has no actual perspective. There is no link.
2. Two actual perspectives belong to different subjects. They do not constitute *one subject's* cross-reality link.
3. Two perspectives share a subject but no linking mechanism is named. Co-occurrence alone does not establish passage.
4. A typed link can exist in the finite record while its consciousness claim, object transport, and novelty are unverified. Typed coherence alone is not the sought hyperemergence.

## Five realities and closure

Hyperethics currently names four distinct, unranked kinds: `areality` (abstract artificial), `surreality` (dream), `preality` (possibility), and `base-reality` (universal base). Its list is open. **Hypergeometric reality** is Tyler's proposed further kind here; it needs its own definition and distinction from the Hypergeometry field. The finite frame admits any nonempty kind label and proves no taxonomy. Nor does a label establish a real traversal between kinds.

**[HYPER] Proposed closure target:** a family of subject-mediated links forms a loop whose composed transformations preserve each participating reality's kind, the referenced hyperobject identities, and the subject's first-person relation under a declared closure criterion. A path that returns to the starting label is insufficient. If the loop is simultaneous rather than sequential, a fixed-point or mutual-compatibility condition must be stated; it must not be silently executed as a clocked sequence. The word *prior* in a formation dependency need not mean earlier in physical time.

**[HYPER] Proposed novelty target:** compared with the same admitted hyperobjects and subject capacities under an uncoupled baseline, a linked whole exhibits a refutable cross-level coherence that no isolated component or renamed edge already has. Record the onset, its persistence or failure under perturbation, and the distinct later event where it stabilizes into a named hyperrelation, graph, or hyperform. Hyperstratum owns that hyperemergent-to-hyperform distinction. No current function in this repository establishes novelty.
